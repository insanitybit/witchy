# Independent B1/B7 review, 2026-09-08

Terra reviewed `49cccc721` independently while the integration owner worked
on recursive layouts. This is a review of rule coverage and correspondence
metadata; it does not establish checker or encoder soundness.

## Findings

1. The implementation manifest covers all 39 `CanonicalRuleId` variants, but
   the RFC's v1 kernel uses a different operation vocabulary. There is no
   exhaustive crosswalk identifying which RFC operations are implemented by
   one rule, expanded into several rules, refused, or deferred. Examples:
   `AllocRegion`, `IsUnique`, `Initialize`, `Replace`, `CloneValue`, and
   `MakeClosure`. B1 needs that crosswalk before review can close the row.

2. `ClosureSlot` is a checker subjudgment for callable loads and stores,
   rather than an operation. Its manifest row claims only `MemoryLoad`.
   Actual operations correctly map `Load` to `MemoryLoad` and `Store` to
   `MemoryStore`; the standalone row does not express that relationship.
   B7 needs this row classified as a subjudgment over both mappings.

## Evidence reviewed

- `rfcs/0165-safewir-canonical-ir.md`, exhaustive v1 operation kernel.
- `canonical_safewir/manifest.rs`, exhaustive list and backend equality test.
- `canonical_safewir.rs`, `CanonicalRuleId` and operation classification.
- `canonical_wasmir.rs`, `rule_backend`, `operation_rule`, and
  `check_correspondence`.

Every actual operation/terminator has a correspondence entry checked for
presence, uniqueness, payload, and category. `Retain` is explicitly refused.
Neither finding by itself establishes unsound execution. Instruction offsets,
widths, signedness, trap order, and complete memory effects were not reviewed
by this pass. B1/B7 remain open until the crosswalk/mapping corrections and
their independent review are recorded.
